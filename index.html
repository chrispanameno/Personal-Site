About

A Voice Shaped
by Two Worlds

Chris Panameno is a saxophonist whose playing moves fluidly between the warmth of classic jazz and the rhythmic pulse of Latin music. Raised between two cultures and two musical traditions, he has developed a voice that is unmistakably his own — expressive, considered, and deeply felt.

Over more than a decade of performance, Chris has appeared at celebrated venues across the country, collaborated with ensembles at the forefront of contemporary jazz, and contributed to recordings spanning jazz, R&B, and Latin music. His playing has been described as both technically impeccable and emotionally immediate.

Whether performing for an intimate gathering or a grand occasion, Chris brings the same commitment: music that stays with you long after the last note fades.

Offerings

Work With Chris

Live Performance

From intimate private dinners to large-scale galas, weddings, and concert engagements — Chris delivers a live experience tailored precisely to your event. Configurations range from solo and duo to full ensemble, accommodating any setting or atmosphere.

Studio & Session Work

Chris brings a refined and versatile studio presence to any recording project. Whether your production calls for a lyrical ballad tone, a commanding bebop statement, or something entirely singular, he will find the sound your music demands.

Private Instruction

Dedicated to the continuity of the art, Chris offers private lessons to students at every stage — from those just beginning their relationship with the instrument to advanced players seeking to deepen their musical language and interpretive range.
