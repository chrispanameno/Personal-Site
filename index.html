A Voice in Every Note

With over a decade of performance experience, Chris Panameno brings a rich, soulful voice to the saxophone that has moved audiences from intimate jazz clubs to sweeping concert stages. His playing draws from classic jazz, contemporary R&B, and the warm Latin rhythms of his heritage — creating a sound that is immediately recognisable and deeply personal.

Chris has shared the stage with celebrated ensembles across the country and has contributed to recordings that span jazz, pop, and everything in between. Whether leading his own quartet or appearing as a featured soloist, he brings precision, instinct, and an unmistakable warmth to every performance.

Music, for Chris, is a conversation — and he invites you to be part of it.
